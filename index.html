ABOUT THE PROJECT

Digital Evidence.
Deeper Insight.

DeepTrace is an AI-powered forensic analysis project designed to investigate suspected face-swap deepfake videos. It combines CNN-based video classification with temporal analysis, visual explainability, and supporting forensic measurements.

The system is designed to help users examine digital content more systematically while acknowledging the limitations of automated detection.
